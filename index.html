The Mayfly Mutiny

A dystopian science fiction novel about power, faith and resistance in a Martian colony built on rigid inequality. When caregiver Marnette Croft begins to question the truths she has always lived by, she finds herself pulled toward a dangerous movement that could change the future of the colony — if it doesn't destroy her first.

Chilli Racers

Coming Summer 2026

A near-future dystopian thriller about seduction, mentorship, and the cost of authenticity. In a world where internal combustion engines are banned and medical implants can pull you back from death, Flynn is offered something rarer than either: the feeling of being truly alive. All it will cost him is everything he thought he was.

Carrier to Yesterday

Coming Autumn 2026

A science fiction novel about curiosity, chance, and consequences, as two students chase an obscure wrinkle in electromagnetic theory that seems to let a radio signal arrive before it's sent. When their late-night experiments start producing results that shouldn't be possible, they're drawn into a risky attempt to turn physics into an advantage — only to discover that borrowing time comes with a price they never calculated.
